# Logo options

Three candidates for the plugin icon (Kino palette: red #E50914, dark #0D0D0D, white). Each has its SVG source and
PNG renders at 512, 192 and 48 px. Variant A is the provisional `icon.svg` / `icon.png` until the owner picks one;
this folder goes away after that choice.

- **A** `a-page-film`: a news page whose left edge is a film strip, with a headline and a play button.
- **B** `b-clapper-news`: a clapperboard whose slate is a news page (headline, text lines, folded corner).
- **C** `c-newsreel`: a newsreel, a strip of film whose frames hold headlines instead of pictures.
